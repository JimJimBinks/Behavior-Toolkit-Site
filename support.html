Support

Need help with Behavior Toolkit? Send a message to support@behaviortoolkit.app.

What to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The app version, if available.
  • What you were trying to do.
  • What happened instead.
  • Whether iCloud sync, Care Circle, Sound Monitor, or exports were involved.

Privacy note

Please avoid sending sensitive details unless they are necessary to understand the issue. Screenshots may contain private behavior, medication, sleep, or child information.

Emergency and care decisions

Behavior Toolkit is not an emergency service and does not provide medical, behavioral, therapeutic, or educational advice. For urgent safety or medical concerns, contact emergency services or a qualified professional.
