Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Privacy at a glance

  • Your records are stored on your device. Optional syncing, sharing, exports, and device backups can create copies elsewhere.
  • Optional iCloud and Care Circle features use Apple's services. The developer does not receive your records automatically or use them for advertising or tracking.
  • Microphone audio is analyzed on-device, without saving or uploading recordings. Session records generated from detections can sync like other records.
  • Exported backups, CSV files, and PDFs are not encrypted or password-protected by the app. Share them only with people and services you trust.

Who this policy covers

Behavior Toolkit is a tracking and communication tool for parents, caregivers, and trusted adults. You control the information you enter and the people you choose to share it with. The developer does not operate a server that receives your app records, sell those records, or use them for advertising or tracking.

Information the app handles

The app stores information you enter or generate while using its features. This can include child profile names, behavior sessions, Quick Log occurrences, timestamps and durations, notes, medication changes and dose logs, sleep, observations, context, caregiver responses, life events, settings, and generated summaries. These records may contain sensitive family or health information.

Device storage and optional iCloud sync

Records are stored on your device. The app and its widgets share necessary information through an Apple App Group container. Your device's security, passcode, backup settings, and Apple's platform protections help protect that storage.

When iCloud is available and syncing is enabled, records can sync through Apple's CloudKit service across your devices. The app writes synced record contents to CloudKit encrypted fields. CloudKit manages this encryption and account access. Operational information such as record identifiers, update timestamps, sharing metadata, and notification event types is separate from the encrypted record contents. Cloud encryption does not encrypt files you export from the app.

Apple's handling of iCloud and device backups is governed by Apple's policies and your Apple account settings. The developer does not receive your private iCloud records merely because you use the app.

Care Circle

Care Circle uses Apple's CloudKit sharing to let you share selected records with caregivers you invite. Invited caregivers can access shared information and, where their permissions allow, make changes. Review who has access and invite only people you trust. Ending sharing cannot recall files, screenshots, or other copies someone already made.

Optional microphone monitoring

Sound Monitor requests microphone permission before analyzing live audio for selected sounds. Analysis uses Apple's on-device SoundAnalysis framework. The app does not save sound recordings or upload microphone audio.

A detection can start or update a behavior session. The resulting session records, including timing and duration, may be saved, synced through iCloud or Care Circle, and included in exports. You can stop monitoring and revoke microphone permission in system Settings.

On-device summaries

On supported devices, optional Apple Intelligence features use Apple's on-device model to summarize notes and patterns. The app does not send these prompts to a developer-operated or third-party AI server. Saved records and summaries may still be included in the app's normal sync or user-chosen exports. Review generated summaries for mistakes.

Widgets and notifications

Care Circle notifications are delivered through Apple's CloudKit subscription and push notification services. Behavior Toolkit does not operate a separate notification server. Messages such as “Behavior timer started” do not include child names or session notes. Local note reminders also use generic messages.

Live Activity timers are created and updated on your device. Widgets can display child names, selected behaviors, and recent logging feedback. These surfaces may be visible to someone who can see your screen. Review your widget, notification-preview, and Lock Screen settings.

Backups, exports, and recovery copies

Warning: exported backups, CSV files, and PDF summaries are not encrypted or password-protected by the app. Anyone with access to a file may be able to read it.

Exports may include sensitive information about multiple children. Store and share them only through destinations you trust. The chosen destination's protections and policies apply.

The app keeps local last-good and recovery copies to help prevent data loss. Temporary export copies can remain on the device until cleaned up by the app or operating system. Removing an item from the current app records does not automatically remove it from earlier backups, recovery copies, exports, or recipients' copies.

How long information is kept

The app does not automatically expire your saved records after a set number of days. Current records remain until you delete them or remove the relevant app or cloud data. Local recovery copies are retained to help prevent data loss; they do not have a scheduled expiry. Temporary export files may remain until the app or operating system cleans them up. Files you save or share elsewhere remain subject to that destination's retention and deletion controls.

Deleting records and managing sharing

Use the app's supported delete controls to remove current entries. When sync is available, supported deletions propagate to synced records. The app can retain identifiers marking deleted entries so that another device does not restore them during sync.

Deleting an entry does not erase it from earlier backups, recovery copies, or exported files. Ending Care Circle sharing does not recall copies another person already saved. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove cloud records or exported files.

Manage Care Circle access with its sharing controls and manage iCloud storage through your Apple account settings. Contact us if you need help understanding these controls. Please avoid sending sensitive family records with a support request.

Analytics, diagnostics, and support

The app contains no third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDK. Apple's optional diagnostics and TestFlight features are governed by Apple's settings and policies. If you contact support or voluntarily send feedback, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to address your request. Avoid including sensitive family information in support messages or attachments when it is not necessary.

Children and medical information

The app is intended for caregivers and trusted adults, not for children to submit information independently. It does not diagnose, treat, predict, or replace professional care. Work with qualified professionals when making care decisions.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact privacy@behaviortoolkit.app. We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and identify the effective date of the updated policy.
