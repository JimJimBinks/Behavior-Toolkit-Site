Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Behavior Toolkit is designed for caregivers who may enter sensitive family information. Privacy, dignity, and caregiver control are central to the app.

Information you enter

The app may store information you choose to enter, such as behavior sessions, timestamps, duration, notes, context, medication changes, medication dose logs, sleep entries, observations, child profile names, app settings, and exported summaries.

Health-related information

Behavior sessions, medication changes, dose logs, sleep entries, and observations may be health-related. We use this information only to provide the app's tracking, review, and sharing features that you choose. We do not use it for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and we do not share it with third parties except when you choose to share it.

Where app data is stored

Behavior Toolkit stores app data on your device. If iCloud sync is turned on and available, the app may use Apple iCloud and CloudKit to sync your app data across devices signed in to your Apple account. Synced data is stored in your private iCloud account. Apple's handling of that data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Care Circle sharing

If you choose to use Care Circle sharing, selected app data may be shared through Apple's CloudKit sharing features with trusted contacts you invite. Shared data is stored in your private iCloud account, and invited contacts can access it only through that sharing. Shared information may include behavior sessions, medication, sleep, notes, active timer state, and app settings. Only invite people you trust. You control whether you set up sharing, and you can stop sharing at any time, which ends the access of the people you invited.

Microphone and Sound Monitor

Sound Monitor is an optional feature. Behavior Toolkit asks for microphone permission before Sound Monitor can listen, and it uses the microphone only to analyze live audio for the sound patterns you select. All of this analysis happens on your device. Audio is never sent off your device. You can turn Sound Monitor off or revoke microphone access at any time in iOS Settings.

Exports and sharing

The app may let you create CSV files or PDF summaries. You decide whether to share those files. Email and text messages may not be encrypted, so sharing through a provider portal or other secure method may be safer for sensitive information.

Data selling and advertising

Behavior Toolkit does not sell your data. The app is not designed for advertising or third-party tracking, and it does not use your information to target advertising.

Analytics and diagnostics

Behavior Toolkit does not collect analytics about how you use the app, and it does not include third-party advertising, tracking, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. The analytics screens in the app summarize your behavior data entirely on your device. If the app adds any service that collects data, this policy will be updated before that change is released.

Website and email signup

If you ask to be notified when the app is ready, you can enter your email address on our website. That form is handled by Buttondown, which sends the notification emails on our behalf. We use your email address only to send updates about Behavior Toolkit. You can unsubscribe from any email at any time.

Data retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Data synced through iCloud or shared through Care Circle remains there until it is deleted or sharing is stopped. Email signup details are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Deleting your data

You can delete data from within the app where supported. Data synced through iCloud or shared through Care Circle may also be affected by your Apple iCloud settings and CloudKit sharing controls. To ask us to delete your email signup details, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Behavior Toolkit is intended for use by parents, caregivers, and trusted adults. It is not intended for children to submit information directly without caregiver involvement.

Medical disclaimer

Behavior Toolkit is a tracking and communication tool. It does not diagnose, treat, predict, prevent, or replace professional care.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@behaviortoolkit.app
